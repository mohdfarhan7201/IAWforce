Gurkha Capabilities

Ground Clearance

With best-in-segment ground clearance at 233mm, the Force Gurkha is your ultimate Adventure vehicle.

Water Wading Capability

The air intake snorkel gives the All New Force Gurkha a high water-wading ability of up to 700 mm which is unmatched by any other vehicle in this category.

Approach/departure angle

Experience unmatched capability with Gurkha, featuring a remarkable 39° approach angle, a 37° departure angle and 28° rampover angle.

Gradeability

This 4x4x4 vehicle has an unrivalled gradeability of 35° to take on extreme gradients.

Maneuverability

This 4x4x4 vehicle has the lowest-in-class turning radius of 5.50 meters.
