e-Traveller Smart Citibus EV

The Force e-Traveller SMART CITIBUS is India’s first monocoque panel e-Van, designed as a 14-seater electric mobility solution for last-mile connectivity. Compact, fully electric and robustly engineered, it suits both city bus operations and employee transport. Built to support global efforts toward reducing carbon footprint and promoting a greener environment, it runs on a 100% electric drivetrain with zero emissions. The monocoque body design offers higher ride comfort, while the Cathodic Electro-Deposition (CED) painting process adds long-term body durability and gloss retention. Its compact size supports faster turnaround and effortless access across dense city routes.

  • Compact 5.4-meter length navigates inner-city routes with ease

  • Best-in-class manoeuvrability with the smallest turning circle

  • Smaller footprint reduces congestion and speeds filling-up

  • Lightweight monocoque body cuts power use and costs

  • Faster turnaround enables more trips per shift
